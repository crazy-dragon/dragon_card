Chinese Idioms / 成语积累
=========================

10 common four-character idioms with pinyin, meaning and example
sentence, plus a companion web tool.

10 条常用四字成语，带拼音、释义与例句，配套网页小工具。

Using the tool / 使用小工具
---------------------------
1. Import the deck, then import tool.zip and bind it to this deck
   （导入卡组后导入 tool.zip 并绑定到本卡组）.
2. Two views: idiom wall (grid of big serif idioms + pinyin + meaning;
   click a cell to hear the idiom and jump to its card) and cards.
   （两种视图：成语墙——衬线大字 + 拼音 + 释义，点格子读成语并跳卡；
   以及卡片。）
3. Tap the idiom or the example sentence to hear it read aloud.
   （点成语或例句可点读。）
