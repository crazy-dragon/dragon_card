Chinese Idiom Card Deck
=============================

Learn Chinese four-character idioms: idiom, pinyin, meaning, example.

How to use
----------
1. Create a deck, pick the Chinese Idiom Card template
2. Import cards.json (sample) or your own idiom data
3. Tap the eye button to toggle the meaning

------------------------------------------------------------

Chinese Idiom Card 成语卡组
==============================

学习中文四字成语：成语、拼音、释义、例句。

使用
----
1. 新建卡组，选择 Chinese Idiom Card 模板
2. 导入数据 cards.json（示例数据）或自有成语数据
3. 点击眼睛按钮切换释义显示
